Ncell, Nepal's private mobile operator, will shut down its 3G mobile internet service completely by December 17. If your parents or relatives in Nepal still use an older Ncell 3G SIM for Viber, WhatsApp or Messenger calls with you in the US, their mobile data could stop working within weeks.

## Shutdown in four phases

According to [Nepal News](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/business/nepal-news-evening-economic-brief-november-19-2026/), 992 of Ncell's 3G sites are already off. The remaining 2,137 will be switched off in four phases between November 23 and December 12, with the whole process finished by December 17. Ncell says 4G now covers about 95% of Nepal's population.

## Calls still work, data may not

According to [Ncell's official FAQ](https://www.ncell.com.np/en/ncell-shutting-down-3g), regular voice calls are not affected. Voice and SMS keep working on the 2G and 4G networks. The change hits mobile data:

- **3G SIM in a 4G/VoLTE phone:** Swap it for a free 4G SIM at any authorized Ncell point of sale or Ncell Centre. Customers who switch also get 2GB of 4G data valid for seven days.
- **3G SIM in an older phone without 4G:** Calls and texts work, but mobile internet will not unless the user gets both a 4G SIM and a VoLTE-capable phone.
- **4G SIM in a VoLTE phone:** Data is not affected. Ncell says users can activate VoLTE by dialing `*123*11#` and turning VoLTE on in the phone's settings.

Ncell takes SIM swap questions at 9005. Its list of VoLTE-capable phones is on [Ncell's website](https://www.ncell.com.np/en/individual/offers/volte).

## Nepal Telecom is cutting 3G too

According to [Kantipur](https://ekantipur.com/business/2026/11/05/ncell-to-shut-down-3g-within-a-month-telecom-to-remove-1000-sites-in-current-fiscal-year-53-19.html), state-owned Nepal Telecom plans to switch off 1,000 of its 3G sites this fiscal year. It has not set a date for ending 3G entirely.

## What to do

If family members in Nepal — especially older parents — use Ncell, ask them now to check whether their SIM is 3G or 4G and whether their phone supports 4G. If it is an old SIM, swapping it at an Ncell center before December 17 will keep video calls and messages from suddenly stopping.
