The U.S. State Department has released the **December 2026 Visa Bulletin**. Published on November 27, it moves several family-sponsored dates noticeably forward for applicants from India and the Philippines, but dates for Nepal-born applicants are unchanged. U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) has confirmed that people filing Form I-485 for a green card from inside the United States in December must use the "Dates for Filing" chart.

## A big jump in F4

F4 is the category for brothers and sisters of U.S. citizens. On the Dates for Filing chart, India's F4 date moves from February 1, 2007 to **August 15, 2007** — roughly six and a half months. The Philippines' F4 date moves from March 22, 2009 to **November 15, 2009** — about eight months.

Final Action Dates moved too: India's F4 goes from December 15, 2006 to February 15, 2007, and the Philippines' F4 from May 15, 2008 to **May 15, 2009** — a full year for the Philippines. Mexico's F3 final action date also advances one year, to July 15, 2002.

## What it means for Nepali applicants

Applicants born in Nepal fall under the general "All Chargeability Areas" chart, and that chart shows **no change** for December. Filing dates remain February 1, 2021 for F1, September 1, 2020 for F2B, November 1, 2015 for F3 and **November 1, 2012** for F4. The F4 final action date also stays at October 22, 2012.

There is one bright spot: the F2A filing date — for spouses and minor children of green card holders — is **current for all countries**, meaning eligible applicants can file now. The F2A final action date also moves one month ahead, to November 22, 2026.

## Employment categories unchanged

Employment-based filing dates do not change in December. EB-2 stays at March 15, 2026 for all countries and EB-3 at August 1, 2024.

## Why the dates moved — and a warning

The State Department explained that recent presidential proclamations have slowed visa issuance, so it advanced dates to make sure fiscal year 2027 numbers get used. But the bulletin also warns that dates could **move backward (retrogress)** in coming months, and that some categories could become "Unavailable" before the fiscal year ends.

If your priority date is earlier than the new cutoff, you can file an I-485 in December. Check the chart for your own category and country of birth, because dates differ by country.
