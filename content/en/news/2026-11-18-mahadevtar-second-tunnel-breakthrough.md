The second bore of the Mahadevtar tunnel on Nepal's Kathmandu-Tarai Expressway in Makawanpur broke through on Wednesday. The 3.445-km bore will carry traffic from Nijgadh toward Kathmandu.

According to The Kathmandu Post, President Ramchandra Paudel, Infrastructure Minister Sunil Lamsal and Chief of Army Staff Ashok Raj Sigdel attended the breakthrough.

The other Mahadevtar bore, 3.486 km long, broke through in the third week of August.

## The expressway and its tunnels

The expressway runs 70.977 km from Khokana in Lalitpur to Nijgadh in Bara and includes 11.2 km of tunnels in total. Status of the other tunnels:

- **Dhedre** (1,691 m) and **Len Danda** (1,623 m): broken through, finishing work underway
- **Chandram Bhir** (2,237 m): 780 m excavated
- **Devichaur** (1,087 m): 123 m completed
- **Sisautar** (520 m): 53 m excavated
- **Mauribhir** (583 m): portal work underway

## Half done after nine years

The Nepali Army was given the job of building the expressway on Aug. 11, 2017. It was originally due to finish in December 2024. The deadline was pushed to mid-April 2027 and then again to mid-April 2030.

After nine years, the project is only about **50% complete**. Slow construction, land disputes and delays in clearing trees are cited as causes.

## Cost and land

The project's estimated cost is **Rs 211.93 billion** including VAT. According to the project, Rs 94.22 billion had been spent by mid-September.

The expressway is planned to have 89 bridges. Contracts have been awarded for 85 and construction is underway; 5 are already built, while contracts for 4 bridges in the Khokana section have not been finalized.

Still to be acquired are 69,186 square meters of private land and 7,122 square meters of guthi (trust) land. Another 83,939 square meters has not yet been valued.
