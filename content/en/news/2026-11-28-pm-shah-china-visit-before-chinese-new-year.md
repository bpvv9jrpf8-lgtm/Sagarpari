Fresh from his state visit to India, Prime Minister **Balendra Shah** is now preparing for a trip to China. Kathmandu and Beijing are discussing an official visit that could take place before the **Chinese New Year on Feb. 6**, The Kathmandu Post reported Saturday.

## What we know

According to the Post, ministers and Foreign Ministry officials have signaled that the timing depends on the dates Beijing proposes. No date has been set yet.

"We are waiting for the dates from the Chinese side," a minister told the Post. The same minister, who was not named, said the prime minister is keen to visit China after his India trip, which officials described as successful.

## The ambassador's message

Before Shah's India visit, Chinese Ambassador **Zhang Maoming** met Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle, Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal, Foreign Secretary Amrit Bahadur Rai and other officials. According to the Post, he conveyed that Beijing was ready to welcome Shah at the earliest possible date, but he did not share specific dates or details.

The Indian news agency IANS reported that the Foreign Ministry has begun preliminary preparations and that officials hope Beijing will extend an invitation sometime in January. Neither report confirms that a formal invitation has already been sent.

## Background

Shah visited India on Nov. 22 and 23, where the two countries signed several agreements. Separately, a 20-member delegation from the Rastriya Swatantra Party (RSP), led by General Secretary **Bipin Acharya**, visited China from Nov. 15 to 26 at the invitation of the Communist Party of China, traveling to Beijing, Chengdu and Hangzhou.

RSP President **Rabi Lamichhane**'s own China trip had been planned for September but was postponed indefinitely after the Bhotekoshi floods.

More details are expected once the dates and agenda of the visit are announced.
