Nepal now has a tentative timeline for its next local and provincial elections. **The Election Commission has proposed holding local elections (rural municipalities and municipalities) in Baisakh 2084 and provincial assembly elections in Mangsir 2084.** On the Western calendar, that is roughly April–May 2027 and November–December 2027.

The commission presented the possible schedule on Friday, November 20 (Mangsir 4), at a meeting with top leaders of the major political parties. It is only a proposal for now; no election dates have been set.

## Why now?

Current local representatives were elected on Baisakh 30, 2079 (May 2022), and provincial assembly members on Mangsir 4, 2079 (November 2022). By law, a village or municipal assembly serves five years from the date of its election. According to Nagarik daily, local terms end on **Baisakh 30, 2084**, leaving about five months. That is why the commission has started consulting parties to prepare for new elections.

## Who was at the meeting?

According to Onlinekhabar English, participants included Rastriya Swatantra Party chair Rabi Lamichhane, Nepali Congress president Gagan Thapa and NCP coordinator Pushpa Kamal Dahal "Prachanda," among others. CPN-UML chair KP Sharma Oli arrived but left, asking the party's election department chief, Devendra Dahal, to attend in his place.

Prachanda's secretariat said he found the commission's proposal "reasonable," but his party will hold further internal discussion and study before announcing its final position. Other parties have not made formal positions public.

## Voting rights for Nepalis abroad

For Nepalis living in the US and elsewhere, another issue raised at the same meeting stands out. **Prachanda called for guaranteeing the right to vote for Nepali citizens living abroad.**

According to his secretariat, Prachanda:

- Said he has pushed for this since before the first Constituent Assembly election.
- Said the technical and legal aspects need serious consideration.
- Suggested the government and the Election Commission work together on bilateral talks with countries that have large Nepali populations.
- Said overseas voting would strengthen the credibility of elections and protect citizens' right to take part in democratic decisions.

In October, the Election Commission sent a proposal to the Home Ministry to include inter-district and external (overseas) voting in the Election Management Bill. No law has been passed yet, so it remains unclear whether Nepalis abroad will be able to vote in the upcoming local elections.

The commission is expected to announce final dates after further talks with the parties.
