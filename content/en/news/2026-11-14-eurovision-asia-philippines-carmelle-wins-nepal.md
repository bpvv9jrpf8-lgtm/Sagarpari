Filipino singer CARMELLE has won the first-ever Eurovision Song Contest Asia. At the grand final on Saturday, Nov. 14, at One Bangkok Forum in Bangkok, Thailand, she took first place with "Sobra-Sobra" and a total of 211 points. Nepal, competing for the first time, finished near the bottom of the scoreboard.

## Carmelle led both votes

According to the European Broadcasting Union (EBU), Carmelle received 93 points from the national juries and 118 from the public vote, topping both.

Host country Thailand came second with the duo MANG x DAOU and "Aroma" on 170 points. Vietnam's Tóc Tiên was third with 164 points. Malaysia and Laos rounded out the top five.

AFP reported that Malaysia's Aina Abdul was an early favorite with the juries, but Carmelle pulled well ahead once the public vote came in. Speaking to reporters, Carmelle called it "such an unexpected journey."

## Nepal's debut

Nepal was represented by singer-songwriter Pratik Himal Rai with his own song, "Ananta Maya," performed 10th in the running order. According to AFP, Nepal, Cambodia and Bangladesh finished at the bottom of the points table. The EBU's release lists only the top five, so Nepal's exact placing and points have not been officially published yet.

Eleven countries took part this year: Thailand, South Korea, Vietnam, the Philippines, Bangladesh, Mongolia, Nepal, Laos, Bhutan, Malaysia and Cambodia. The winner was decided by a 50/50 split of national jury votes and the public vote. The EBU said viewers in 183 countries and territories, including the 11 participants, voted.

## "Just the beginning"

Martin Green, director of the Eurovision Song Contest, congratulated Carmelle and the Philippines on becoming the contest's "first-ever winners." Executive producer Assaf Blecher called it "just the beginning of an exciting new chapter for Eurovision in Asia."
