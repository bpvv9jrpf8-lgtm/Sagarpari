Nepal's ministers have a new order of precedence. President **Ramchandra Paudel** revised the ranking on Thursday on the recommendation of Prime Minister **Balendra Shah**, according to a statement from the Office of the President. The decision was made under **Article 76(9)** of the Constitution, which covers the formation of the Council of Ministers.

The biggest jump belongs to **Sobita Gautam**, Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs. According to *The Kathmandu Post*, she was seventh in the previous order, set on September 10, and is now third. Foreign Minister **Shisir Khanal**, who held third place before, is now fourth.

## The new order

After Prime Minister Shah, the ministers rank as follows:

- **2.** Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle
- **3.** Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Minister Sobita Gautam
- **4.** Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal
- **5.** Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation Minister Biraj Bhakta Shrestha
- **6.** Home Minister Sudan Gurung
- **7.** Physical Infrastructure Development Minister Sunil Lamsal
- **8.** Science, Technology and Innovation Minister Mahabir Pun
- **9.** Women, Children and Social Security Minister Sita Badi
- **10.** Land Management, Cooperatives, Federal Affairs and General Administration Minister Pratibha Rawal
- **11.** Health and Food Safety Minister Nisha Mehta

They are followed, in order, by the ministers for education and sports, tourism, communications (Dr. Bikram Timilsina), agriculture, forests and environment (Gita Chaudhary), industry (Deepak Kumar Sah) and labour (Ramji Yadav). The cabinet has 17 members including the prime minister.

## No change in portfolios

The change affects only the order of precedence. Neither source reports any minister switching ministries. The ranking decides where ministers sit and stand at government programs, meetings and official ceremonies.

The President's Office statement did not give a reason for the change. Shah's government was formed in late March.
