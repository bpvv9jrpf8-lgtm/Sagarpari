An important form deadline is coming up for anyone preparing to apply for a T visa (T nonimmigrant status), the U.S. immigration benefit for victims of human trafficking. According to a November 9 update from Francis Law Center, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) will **stop accepting older editions of Form I-914 on November 27, 2026**. After that date, an application filed on an outdated edition will be rejected outright, no matter how strong the case is.

## Which editions are accepted?

Starting November 27, USCIS will accept only two editions of Form I-914: the **September 14, 2026 (09/14/26)** edition or the **January 20, 2025 (01/20/25)** edition. Any older edition submitted after that date will be rejected.

The edition date is printed at the bottom of every page of the form. It is easy to miss if you are working from an old download or a copy you printed earlier, so applicants are advised to check the date at the bottom of each page before mailing.

## Every page must be the same edition

USCIS warns that all pages of a single application must come from the same accepted edition. Mixing pages from different editions, or leaving pages out, can also lead to rejection.

A rejected application keeps no filing date and cannot be appealed. The whole packet has to be prepared and sent again. In a sensitive case like a T visa, that kind of delay can affect a victim's ability to stay and to get work authorization.

## Eligibility has not changed

This change does not alter who qualifies for a T visa. It is purely about form compliance: the eligibility rules are the same as before, but USCIS will be strict about which edition of the form you use.

If you plan to file after November 27, download a fresh copy from the official USCIS website rather than relying on a saved copy.

The T visa is a temporary immigration benefit for victims of severe forms of human trafficking. It allows victims to remain in the United States and, in certain situations, to include family members.
