Getting a first U.S. passport may soon be easier for Nepalis who have become American citizens. The State Department is launching a pilot on Monday, **Nov. 16**, that lets some first-time applicants submit **Form DS-11** online.

The notice is scheduled for publication in the Federal Register on Nov. 16. According to Nextgov/FCW, the pilot will run through **Nov. 20, 2027** — roughly one year.

## Who qualifies?

Until now, only people eligible to renew with Form DS-82 could apply online. First-time applicants had to apply in person with their paperwork.

In its first phase, the pilot is open only to:

- Adults in the United States applying for their first passport who can prove U.S. citizenship with a **Certificate of Naturalization**.
- Adults in the United States who previously held a valid U.S. passport but are not eligible to renew with a DS-82.

The department says it may later add applicants using U.S. birth certificates, Certificates of Citizenship or Consular Reports of Birth Abroad, as well as eligible applicants overseas. They are not eligible yet.

## How it works

The application will be available through **America.gov**. Applicants first answer a series of questions to check whether they qualify. Those who do are sent to **Login.gov** to verify their identity.

Applicants then upload a passport photo, choose the passport product and delivery method, and record a video of themselves reciting the required oath that their information is accurate. A passport agent reviews the recording and the rest of the application.

The reports we reviewed did not give details on fees, how the naturalization certificate is submitted or returned, or whether the number of applications is capped. Check the official notice in the Federal Register and travel.state.gov before applying.

## Why it matters

The department processed roughly **13.9 million** DS-11 applications in fiscal year 2025, and the paper form takes an average of 85 minutes to complete. It expects that taking some applications online will save applicants time.

For many newly naturalized Nepali Americans, the pilot could be useful. Because it is a test program, the rules and eligibility may change.
