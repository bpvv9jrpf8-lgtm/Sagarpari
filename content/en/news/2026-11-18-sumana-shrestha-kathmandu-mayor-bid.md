Former education minister Sumana Shrestha has announced she will run for mayor of Kathmandu Metropolitan City in Nepal's upcoming local elections. She made the announcement in a Facebook post, The Kathmandu Post reported.

"I announce my candidacy for mayor of Kathmandu Metropolitan City in the upcoming local elections," she wrote.

## No party yet

Shrestha resigned as joint general secretary of the Rastriya Swatantra Party (RSP) on April 17 and is currently not affiliated with any party. She has not said whether she will run as an independent or on another party's ticket.

The Post's report does not give a date for the local elections.

## Parliament and ministry experience

Shrestha was elected to the House of Representatives through the RSP's proportional representation list. She became education minister on March 6, 2024. As a lawmaker she pushed for a fixed parliamentary calendar and was active on the Education, Health and Information Technology Committee.

She is a permanent resident of Ward 26 in Kathmandu.

## Educated in the US

Her background may interest Nepalis in the United States. Shrestha holds a bachelor's degree in economics from Bryn Mawr College and a bachelor's in mathematics from Haverford College, and an MBA from the MIT Sloan School of Management.

In her announcement, she said she wants to dedicate the knowledge, skills and experience she has gained to Kathmandu's prosperity and good governance.

## What's next

Kathmandu's mayor is one of the most closely watched local offices in Nepal. The field will become clearer once an election date is set and parties name their candidates. SagarPari will update this story as more details emerge.
