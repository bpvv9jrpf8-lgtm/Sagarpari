Nepal's government will take a concessional loan of about $107 million from the International Monetary Fund. The cabinet decided Friday to accept the loan under the IMF's **Rapid Credit Facility (RCF)**.

Communications Minister Bikram Timilsina briefed reporters on the decisions after the meeting. Republica described the loan as interest-free and concessional. The government has not said what the money will be used for.

## Chinese grant for Bhotekoshi flood victims

The cabinet also accepted a $2 million grant from the Chinese government. The money is for emergency rescue and relief after the flash flood on the Bhotekoshi River.

## PM's India visit approved

The cabinet approved Prime Minister Balendra Shah's visit to India on November 22 and 23 and finalized the Nepali delegation that will travel with him. The names of delegation members were not released.

## Survey of government land

Ministers also ordered a nationwide survey of government, public and community land that has not yet been measured, with records to be prepared. No agency or deadline was announced.

## Three policies approved

The cabinet also approved:

- The National Biodiversity Strategy and Action Plan (2026–2030)
- Norms on the conservation of historical, archaeological and cultural heritage (2083 in the Nepali calendar)
- The National Suspension Bridge Policy (2083 in the Nepali calendar)

For Nepalis abroad, the IMF loan and the foreign grant offer a window into the government's finances and its recovery from the floods.
