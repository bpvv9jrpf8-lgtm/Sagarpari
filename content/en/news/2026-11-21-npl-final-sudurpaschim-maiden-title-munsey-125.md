**Sudurpaschim Royals** are Nepal Premier League (NPL) champions for the first time. In Saturday's final at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, they crushed defending champions **Lumbini Lions by 10 wickets**.

According to The Kathmandu Post, Sudurpaschim chased down the 156-run target without losing a wicket in just 13.3 overs, with 39 balls to spare.

## Munsey's blitz

The hero was Scotland's veteran batter **George Munsey**, who smashed an **unbeaten 125 off 54 balls**, with eight fours and 12 sixes. He reached his fifty in 28 balls and his hundred in 49. He was dropped on 44.

Fellow opener **Binod Bhandari** finished on 30 not out off 27 balls, with three fours. The pair put on a record opening partnership.

## Lumbini's innings

Batting first, Lumbini made 155 for six in 20 overs, adding 47 runs in the last five overs.

- Dilip Nath: 39 not out off 31 balls (three sixes)
- Captain Rohit Kumar Paudel: 30 off 31 balls (run out)
- Niroshan Dickwella: 28 off 17 balls
- US batter Ali Sheikh: 24 off 12 balls (two fours, two sixes)
- D'Arcy Short: 21 off 18 balls

Nath and Ali Sheikh added 33 off 19 balls for the sixth wicket.

For Sudurpaschim, **Scott Kuggeleijn** took two wickets for 25 runs in four overs, lifting his tournament tally to 15. Hemant Dhami, Tharindu Ratnayake and captain Dipendra Singh Airee took one wicket each.

## First title in third final

It was Sudurpaschim's third straight final. They lost the first edition's final to Janakpur Bolts and last year's final to Lumbini. They had also lost to Lumbini twice this season, in the league stage and in Qualifier 1. On Saturday, they finally ended the wait with a one-sided win.
