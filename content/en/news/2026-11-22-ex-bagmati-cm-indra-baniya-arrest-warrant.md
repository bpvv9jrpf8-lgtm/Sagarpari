An arrest warrant has been issued for **Indra Baniya**, former chief minister of Nepal's Bagmati Province, in a banking offense case. Onlinekhabar and Ratopati reported the news, citing an official at the police's **Central Investigation Bureau (CIB)**. According to Ratopati, the CIB obtained the warrant from a court last week.

## The allegation

The CIB's preliminary investigation alleges that about **Rs 340 million** in loans from **Prabhu Bank** was spent on purposes other than those the loans were approved for. The loans were taken in the names of Baniya Construction Service Pvt Ltd, Baniya Suppliers Pvt Ltd and a cement company.

Ratopati reports that the money was allegedly kept in other bank accounts and invested in shares. Indra Baniya is a director of Baniya Construction Service. The case is being investigated under Nepal's **Banking Offenses and Punishment Act, 2064 BS**, which makes it an offense to use a loan against its approved purpose.

## Son already in custody

The CIB arrested Baniya's son earlier this month in the same case, and he remains in CIB custody. Ratopati says the former chief minister is linked to the investigation against his son and is currently absconding.

According to Nepal News, police are also looking into Baniya on suspicion of money laundering and have collected records of his financial transactions. Police have not publicly released details. No response from Baniya or his family has been made public so far.

## Who is Indra Baniya?

According to Ratopati, Baniya served as chief minister of Bagmati Province from July 2025 to August 2026. Nepal News describes him as leader of the Nepali Congress parliamentary party in the provincial assembly.

The CIB recently obtained a court warrant in a separate case against former chief secretary Ek Narayan Aryal as well. An arrest warrant is not a finding of guilt; the allegations will be settled only through the investigation and the courts.
