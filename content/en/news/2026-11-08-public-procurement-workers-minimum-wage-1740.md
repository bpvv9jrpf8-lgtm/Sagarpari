Nepal's government has introduced new rules for people who work on government contracts for roads, bridges, buildings and other projects. Under the **Procedure on Remuneration and Social Security of Workers Engaged in Public Procurement, 2083**, these workers must now receive at least **Rs 1,740 a day**, and employers must enroll them in the **Social Security Fund (SSF)**.

## How much workers get

According to news reports, the procedure sets a minimum basic daily wage of **Rs 1,450**. Add the employer's **10 percent** and the government or public body's **10 percent** social security contribution, and the lowest-paid worker's share comes to at least **Rs 1,740**.

For workers at industries, businesses and suppliers that provide goods, the minimum monthly pay is set at **Rs 32,607**. The government's contribution must be included in each project's cost estimate.

## Wages go straight to the bank

The main goal is to stop middlemen from exploiting workers. Wages must now be deposited **directly into the worker's own bank account**. According to Insurance Khabar, no commission can be deducted from wages, and if a contractor fails to pay, the government will deduct the amount from the contractor's payment and send it to the worker's account.

Permanent, temporary, daily-wage and short-term workers must all be covered by the Social Security Fund from their first day on the job. Employers not registered with the fund will not be allowed to take part in public procurement.

## Blacklisting for violations

Firms that break the rules can be barred from public procurement, recommended for blacklisting, lose their deposits and even have their licenses revoked. The procedure also says festival allowances, leave and benefits must be provided under existing law, and that work sites must have safety gear, drinking water, toilets and first aid.

Physical Infrastructure Minister Sunil Lamsal said development should not be limited to building roads, bridges and buildings, and that the rights of the workers who build them must also be guaranteed.

## When it takes effect

According to the reports, the procedure applies to all new contracts announced after it was issued. Contracts already signed will continue under their existing terms.

Many Nepalis living abroad have family members or relatives working in Nepal's construction sector. Those workers can now check whether their wages are reaching their bank accounts and whether they have been enrolled in the Social Security Fund.
