The dates and schedule of Prime Minister Balen Shah's India trip are now official. According to Nepal's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Shah will fly to New Delhi on Sunday, November 22, and return to Kathmandu on November 23. It will be his first official bilateral visit to India as prime minister.

Government officials had earlier said the visit would fall on these dates, but no formal announcement had been made. Foreign Secretary Amrit Kumar Rai shared the details at a press conference on Friday.

## Who is going

A 34-member official delegation will accompany the prime minister. It includes Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle, Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal, the prime minister's chief adviser Kumar Byanjankar and other senior government officials. Another 52 people will travel separately at their own expense, The Kathmandu Post reported. Shah will fly on a Nepal Airlines flight.

On Thursday, the Post reported that Shah is likely to skip the customary guard of honour and airport send-off when he departs.

## What's on the schedule

According to the foreign ministry, during the visit Shah will:

- Pay a courtesy call on Indian President Droupadi Murmu.
- Hold delegation-level talks with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Modi will host a luncheon in Shah's honour, and both leaders will address the media after the talks.
- Receive a courtesy call from India's External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar on Sunday.
- Attend a business summit in New Delhi meant to bring the two countries' business communities together.

Foreign Secretary Rai said several memoranda of understanding covering different areas of cooperation are expected to be signed. Some new projects will be inaugurated, and groundbreaking for others will be announced. The ministry has not said which specific agreements are planned.

The ministry's statement described the trip as "an important opportunity to review our wide-ranging cooperative partnership."

## Background

Earlier reports said Nepal plans to ask India for more than $2 billion in grants and concessional loans during the visit. The two sides are also reported to be preparing a 10-point joint vision document based on the idea of "Prosperous Nepal, Prosperous India." What form those items finally take should become clear during the visit.
