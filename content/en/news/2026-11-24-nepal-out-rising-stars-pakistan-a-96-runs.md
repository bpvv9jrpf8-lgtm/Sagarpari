Nepal's campaign at the Asia Cup Rising Stars in Qatar ended in the group stage. In their final group match on Tuesday, November 24, Nepal lost to Pakistan A by 96 runs.

According to The Kathmandu Post, Nepal were chasing 169 at the University of Doha for Science and Technology ground but were bowled out for 72 in 17.3 overs.

## Rashid Khan's career-best spell

Pakistan A won the toss and batted first, and Nepal's bowlers held them to 168 in 42 overs. Rashid Khan took 5 for 39 in eight overs — his best figures in List A cricket.

Abhishesh Gautam, making his List A debut, took 2 for 27 in nine overs. Captain Sandeep Lamichhane and Kushal Bhurtel took one wicket each.

Pakistan A were in trouble at 36 for three. Nepal then took three wickets for one run to leave them at 67 for six, removing captain Abdul Samad (13), Farhan Yousaf (0) and Mohammad Imran Randhawa (0). But Hasan Nawaz (28) and Ahmed Daniyal (16) added 70 runs, and Rohail Nazir finished unbeaten on 64 off 83 balls with four fours and three sixes.

## Another batting collapse

A modest target proved too much as Nepal's batting crumbled again. Kushal Bhurtel fell for 1 and Dev Khanal for 8. Basir Ahamad made 11 off 19 balls.

Mayan Yadav top-scored with 41 off 51 balls, hitting four fours and two sixes, and was the last man out. Ishan Pandey, Lokesh Bam, Pawan Sarraf, Rashid Khan, Nandan Yadav and captain Sandeep Lamichhane were all out for ducks. Abhishesh Gautam was not out on 1.

For Pakistan A, Mohammad Hasnain and Sufyan Moqim took three wickets each, and Mohammad Imran Randhawa took two.

## How the group finished

Nepal won only one match in the tournament. They were bowled out for 26 against India A in their opener, then bounced back on Sunday with a six-wicket win over the UAE.

Pakistan A topped Group B with three wins from three and advance to the semifinals. India A, with two wins, also go through as runners-up.
