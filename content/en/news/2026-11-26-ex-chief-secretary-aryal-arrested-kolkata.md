Former chief secretary **Ek Narayan Aryal**, who had been out of contact for more than a week, has been arrested in the scam over illegal fees collected from Malaysia-bound Nepali workers in the name of medical screening. According to Onlinekhabar, he was arrested in **Kolkata, India**, and brought to Kathmandu on Thursday.

Earlier on Thursday, a Home Ministry source confirmed the arrest and said more details would be made public later.

## Left for India before the crackdown

An officer at the police **Central Investigation Bureau (CIB)** said Aryal flew from Tribhuvan International Airport to Kolkata on a Buddha Air flight on the morning of **November 17**. The next day, November 18, the CIB arrested two former labour ministers and other suspects. Investigators say Aryal appears to have learned about the operation in advance.

Aryal previously served nearly four years as Nepal's consul general in Kolkata. Investigators suspect he stayed at the home of an Indian acquaintance.

The CIB could not finish his medical check and paperwork in time to present him in court on Thursday. An investigating officer said he will be taken to court on Friday, when police will seek more time to hold him.

## Accused of helping while labour secretary

Investigators say the scheme ran while Aryal was secretary at the Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security, from December 1, 2021 to June 12, 2023. They also suspect he helped the group while he was chief district officer of Kathmandu.

According to the allegations, a syndicate working against the bilateral agreement collected illegal fees from workers through MacroTech Pvt Ltd, owned by Kailash Khadka, converted the money into US dollars and British pounds, and sent it abroad. Investigators have identified **17 transfers** so far, totaling about **Rs 1.69 billion**.

Ten people had already been arrested in the case, including former labour ministers **Rameshwar Rai Yadav** and **Sher Bahadur Kunwar** and former labour secretary **Binod KC**. A court has allowed police to hold them for five more days, a period that ends Monday. With Aryal, 11 people have now been arrested. They are being investigated for fraud and organized crime.

There has been no response yet from Aryal or his lawyers. These are allegations only; no one has been found guilty by a court.

## For workers

If relatives or friends are preparing to work abroad, remind them to get a receipt for every fee and to report any demand above the official rate to Nepal's Department of Foreign Employment or the police.
