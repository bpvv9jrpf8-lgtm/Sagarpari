A three-day **Nepali American Business & Investment Summit (NABIS 2026)** is underway in New York, aiming to bring Nepali American entrepreneurs, investors, policymakers and business leaders together in one place. The Greater New York Nepali Chamber of Commerce is organizing the event with support from the Consulate General of Nepal in New York. It is being held at the Marriott New York JFK Airport hotel in Jamaica, Queens.

According to NepYork, a New York–based Nepali American news site, the summit's theme is "Connecting Capital, Ideas & Opportunities." Organizers hope to make it an annual gathering.

## Day one: startups take the stage

At the opening session on Friday, Nov. 20, Nepal's Consul General in New York, Dadhiram Bhandari, gave welcome remarks. Presentations on business, investment, entrepreneurship and technology followed.

In the afternoon, a two-hour startup and entrepreneur showcase gave founders a chance to pitch their ideas and products and get feedback from investors. The day ended with networking, a welcome reception and dinner.

## Saturday and Sunday program

According to the program, Saturday's official opening ceremony includes a video address by Nepal's Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal and remarks by Deepak Raj Bohara, a member of Nepal's House of Representatives. New York State Assembly member Steven Raga, Maryland state Delegate Harry Bhandari and Mark Jaffe, president and CEO of the Greater New York Chamber of Commerce, are also listed as speakers. Pradip Shrestha, president of the Nepali chamber, is scheduled to give welcome remarks.

Four business panels are planned for Saturday:

- Technology, AI and the future workforce
- Nepal's economic outlook, tourism and emerging business
- Nepal–US trade, investment and capital
- The growth of Nepali businesses in the US

A gala dinner and recognition program are scheduled for Saturday evening.

On Sunday, the program lists a featured address by Basu Maya Tamang, a member of Nepal's House of Representatives and founder of Nepali Man Udyog, followed by business-to-business and business-to-government matchmaking, a closing session and a preview of NABIS 2027.

## Why it matters

Nepali Americans are increasingly visible in US business, from small shops to tech companies. Gatherings like this give new founders a chance to meet investors, build networks with established business owners and learn about investment opportunities in Nepal.

Attendance figures and any investment commitments from the summit have not been made public so far.
