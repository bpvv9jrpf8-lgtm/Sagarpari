India has announced an economic package worth 240 billion Nepali rupees ($1.62 billion) for flood-hit Nepal. Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi made the announcement on Monday in New Delhi after delegation-level talks with Nepal's Prime Minister Balen Shah, Reuters reported.

Nepal had asked India for about $2 billion in support: $1 billion in concessional loans, $500 million in grants and $500 million for reconstruction. India had signaled a positive response before the visit. The $1.62 billion package announced after the talks is somewhat less than what Nepal requested.

## Focus on post-flood rebuilding

The devastating floods of August 26 caused heavy damage across Nepal. According to Reuters, about 1,500 people were killed, and another 5,200 who went missing are presumed dead. Roads, bridges, power infrastructure, farmland and homes were badly damaged.

The package is expected to go toward post-flood reconstruction, road and highway upgrades, hydropower, railways and other infrastructure. Nepal had also sought support for projects such as upgrading the Siddhartha Highway.

## Joint vision document and other agreements

During the talks, the two countries agreed to release a Joint Vision Document titled "Prosperous Nepal, Developed India," which will set long-term goals and concrete plans for bilateral cooperation.

The two sides are set to exchange memorandums of understanding in education, health, agriculture, sports, disaster management and human resource development. The two prime ministers are also scheduled to jointly launch the Saryu–Kamala Express, a weekly train service linking Ayodhya and Janakpurdham.

Nepal also raised its long-standing request for additional air entry routes through Indian airspace. The issue matters to Nepal for the operation of the Bhairahawa and Pokhara international airports.

## Background to the visit

This is Prime Minister Shah's first bilateral foreign visit since he took office in March. He arrived in Delhi on Sunday with a 35-member government delegation and a 52-member business delegation, including Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle and Foreign Minister Shishir Khanal.

After the talks, Modi hosted a lunch in Shah's honor. Shah is also scheduled to meet Indian President Droupadi Murmu, and Indian External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar paid him a courtesy call.
