Three of Nepal's proposed ambassadors have received diplomatic clearance, known as agrément, from their host countries. Nepal's Foreign Ministry confirmed on Friday, Nov. 13, that India, China and Israel had given their approval, OnlineKhabar reported. Clearance for Nepal's nominee to the United States has not yet arrived.

## Who was approved

- **India:** Dr. Nischal Nath Pandey
- **China:** Kalyan Raj Sharma
- **Israel:** Suresh Kumar Karna

According to HimalPress, India sent its consent on Thursday, Nov. 12. The ministry had formally sent Pandey's name to India on Oct. 16, so the answer came in about two weeks. China and Israel had approved their nominees a few days earlier.

## Who is the US nominee?

The government has proposed **Chiranjung Thapa** as ambassador to the United States. The Kathmandu Post describes him as a national security and international affairs specialist with a master's degree in international affairs from Columbia University. He has advised Nepal's Ministry of Defence, National Security Council, the Nepal Army and Nepal Police.

Parliament's hearing committee endorsed Thapa and nine other nominees on Oct. 14. The seven nominees for the US, UK, Germany, South Korea, Spain, Sri Lanka and Oman are still waiting for approval from those countries. No timeline has been made public.

The government recommended ambassadors for 13 countries on Sept. 18. Jigyan Kumar Thapa, the nominee for Japan, withdrew his name on Nov. 2.

## What happens next

Once agrément is granted, Nepal's President formally appoints the ambassadors. Citing sources, OnlineKhabar said the appointments could be made by Monday. After appointment, each ambassador must present credentials to the host country's head of state before starting work.

Nepal News reported that the Foreign Ministry has also begun preparing recommendations for nine other missions that had been led by career diplomats. Ambassador posts in Russia, Australia, Oman and Spain are currently vacant.

## For Nepalis in the US

The Embassy of Nepal in Washington, D.C., handles passports, document attestation and other services for Nepalis living in the US. SagarPari will report once the US grants its approval.
