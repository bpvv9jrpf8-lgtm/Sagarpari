Nepal Army Major General Anupjung Thapa has been promoted to lieutenant general. Nepal's Council of Ministers made the decision at its meeting on Friday, November 20 (Mangsir 4), Annapurna Post and Setokhabar reported.

According to Setokhabar, the Cabinet met at Singha Durbar shortly before Prime Minister Balendra Shah's departure for India.

## Why the post opened up

Annapurna Post reported that Lieutenant General Ganesh Shrestha retires on Mangsir 7, and Thapa was promoted to fill that slot. Thapa's name had been recommended for the promotion on Kartik 11.

## The race for army chief

Nepal News' Friday evening briefing said the promotion makes Thapa the leading contender to become the next Chief of Army Staff.

The current chief, General Ashokraj Sigdel, was appointed on Bhadra 24, 2081 BS, succeeding Prabhuram Sharma. His three-year term ends on Bhadra 24, 2084 BS (September 9, 2027).

Setokhabar reported that if seniority and the promotion order stay as they are, Thapa could take command from Bhadra 25, 2084 BS. But the promotion does not guarantee the top job.

Under Article 267(5) of Nepal's constitution, the President appoints the army chief on the recommendation of the Council of Ministers. The final call will rest with the government of the day.

## Other Cabinet decisions

According to Nepal News, the same Friday meeting also approved Prime Minister Shah's state visit to India. It ordered a survey and record-keeping of government, public and community land, and approved the National Biodiversity Strategy and Action Plan (2026–2030) and the National Suspension Bridge Policy 2026.

Prime Minister Shah leaves Sunday on a two-day state visit to India.
