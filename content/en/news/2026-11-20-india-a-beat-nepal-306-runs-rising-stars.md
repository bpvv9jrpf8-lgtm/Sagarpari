Nepal made a rough start to the Asia Cup Rising Stars (50 overs) tournament in Doha, Qatar. In a Group B match at West End Park International Cricket Stadium on Friday, November 20, India A beat Nepal by 306 runs.

## India A post 332

Nepal won the toss and chose to field. Their bowlers did well early, reducing India A to 42 for 3. Then Kumar Kushagra and captain Tilak Varma put on 206 for the fourth wicket and took the game away.

Kushagra made 117 off 102 balls with 10 fours and 5 sixes and was named player of the match. Tilak Varma scored 107 off 113 balls. A late 36 off 14 balls from Suryansh Shedge lifted India A to 332 for 9 in 50 overs.

Nepal captain Sandeep Lamichhane took 3 for 61 in his 10 overs. According to CricTracker, Lalit Rajbanshi, Nandan Yadav and Pawan Sarraf took two wickets each.

## All out in 11.3 overs

Nepal's chase collapsed completely. They were bowled out for 26 in 11.3 overs. Pawan Sarraf and Rashid Khan top-scored with 6 each, and most of the batters were out for 0 or 1. Five of the 26 runs were extras.

For India A, Prince Yadav took 5 for 6 in 5 overs and Harshit Rana took 4 for 16 in 6 overs. CricTracker called Nepal's total the fifth-lowest in List A cricket history.

## Regulars busy with the NPL

ESPNcricinfo noted that Nepal were without several regular players because the Nepal Premier League (NPL) is still under way. The NPL final between Lumbini Lions and Sudurpaschim Royals is on Saturday.

Group B has India A, Pakistan A, Nepal and the UAE. After one round, India A and Pakistan A have two points each, while Nepal and the UAE have yet to score.
