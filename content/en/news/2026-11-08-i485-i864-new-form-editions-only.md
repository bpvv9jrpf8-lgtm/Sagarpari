If you are getting ready to apply for a green card from inside the United States, the edition date printed on your forms now matters as much as anything in the package. In a November 8 reminder, the New York immigration law firm Berd & Klauss said U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) currently accepts only the **09/18/26 edition of Form I-485** and only the **08/24/26 edition of Form I-864**. The firm warned that packages sent on older editions can be rejected.

## I-485: new edition, no grace period

Form I-485 is the main green card application for people adjusting to permanent residence while in the U.S. USCIS revised the form to line up with its new public charge final rule.

Since September 18, 2026, only the 09/18/26 edition has been accepted. Applications mailed or filed online on or after September 18 using the 01/20/25 or 09/04/26 edition are rejected. There was no grace period for this change.

Because the form was rewritten for the new public charge rule, applicants are advised to read the new instructions in full and not to copy answers over from an old draft.

## I-864: USCIS may check the sponsor's credit information

Form I-864, the Affidavit of Support, is signed by the sponsor in most family-based cases to show they can support the immigrant financially.

USCIS published the 08/24/26 edition on August 31, 2026. The older 10/17/24 edition had a 30-day grace period, but since October 1, 2026, only the new edition is accepted.

The new edition includes a privacy acknowledgment that allows USCIS to request information about the sponsor from one or more consumer reporting agencies. If the sponsor has a credit or security freeze, USCIS may not be able to get that information, so sponsors are told to respond quickly if they are asked to lift a freeze.

Attorney Patrick Klauss said many couples spend weeks putting a package together, and some started before these changes. Now, he said, the edition date at the bottom of every page decides whether the filing is accepted.

## Checklist before you mail

- Make sure every page of Form I-485 shows 09/18/26 and every page of Form I-864 shows 08/24/26.
- Download the forms directly from the official USCIS website on the day you finalize the package.
- Sponsors with a credit freeze should be ready to lift it if USCIS asks.
- Re-check the current filing address and fee on the USCIS form page before you send anything.
