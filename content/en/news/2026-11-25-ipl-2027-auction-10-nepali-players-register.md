Ten Nepali cricketers have registered for the player auction of the 2027 Indian Premier League (IPL), the world's most-watched T20 league. They appear on the preliminary registration list that India's cricket board, the BCCI, made public on Wednesday.

## 1,220 players in the pool

According to the BCCI register, **1,220 players** signed up in total — 939 from India and 281 from 17 other countries.

Among ICC associate nations, the United States has the most registrations with 13. Nepal and the UAE have 10 each, the Netherlands 3, and Canada, Japan and Scotland 2 each.

## Registering is not the same as being picked

Franchises must submit the players they are interested in by December 1. The **final auction list will be released on December 4**, and only then will it be clear how many of the 10 Nepalis made the cut.

The auction itself is set for **December 16 in Goa**, according to Ratopati.

Spots are scarce. The 10 teams have already retained or signed 184 players, so at most 66 players will be bought at the auction, and only 25 of those slots can go to overseas players. Each team can field no more than four foreign players in its playing XI. Because experienced players from Test nations are also in the pool, associate-nation players face a tough climb.

## Only one Nepali so far

Sandeep Lamichhane remains the only Nepali to have played in the IPL. The Delhi Daredevils (now Delhi Capitals) signed him in 2018, and he played for Delhi in the 2018 and 2019 seasons, taking 13 wickets in 9 matches.

The Nepal Premier League (NPL) has since given more Nepali players a bigger stage. For Nepali cricket fans in the US, December 4 and December 16 are the dates to watch.
