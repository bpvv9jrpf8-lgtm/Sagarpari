A Nepali citizen has died in recent attacks in Saudi Arabia, Nepal's government confirmed on Thursday (Nov. 26). In a statement, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs expressed deep sorrow over the death and offered condolences to the family, according to The Kathmandu Post and the news agency IANS.

## Victim not identified

The ministry did not name the Nepali who died, and it did not say when or where the fatal incident happened. It is therefore still unclear whether the death is linked to the Nov. 24 attack in Riyadh.

According to the ministry, three other Nepalis were injured in the attacks. One has since recovered, and the other two are still receiving medical treatment. The ministry wished them a speedy recovery.

## Call for restraint

In the statement, Nepal repeated its position that civilians and public infrastructure must not be targeted under any circumstances. It urged all parties to "exercise maximum restraint" and to resolve disputes through peaceful means.

## Background

On Nov. 24, a drone attack on a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District injured 11 people, including three Nepalis. Saudi Civil Defense blamed Yemen's Houthi movement for the attack. SagarPari [reported on that attack earlier](/en/news/riyadh-tower-houthi-attack-three-nepalis-injured/).

Earlier, on Oct. 21, Nepali worker Bharat Mahato was killed when debris from an intercepted drone fell at Saudi Aramco's Shaybah oil field. According to IANS, Foreign Ministry figures show more than 1.7 million Nepalis live in West Asia, including Saudi Arabia, most of them as migrant workers.

## What families can do

Families worried about relatives in Saudi Arabia can contact the Embassy of Nepal in Riyadh or Nepal's Ministry of Foreign Affairs for official information. Avoid trusting unconfirmed names or details circulating on social media, and wait for official updates.
