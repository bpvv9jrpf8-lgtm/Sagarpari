US magazine TIME has included Nepal's Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle in "TIME100 Climate", its 2026 list of the 100 most influential people in climate. Wagle appears in the "Leaders" category of the list released on Monday, November 9.

## Why Wagle made the list

According to The Kathmandu Post, TIME highlighted Wagle's work to bring Nepal's climate risk and its demand for international compensation to the global stage. TIME pointed to the "climate justice" campaign he led after the devastating glacial flood in August.

After the flood, Nepal asked the world's three largest carbon emitters — China, the US and India — for climate compensation. Reconstruction is estimated to cost about $5 billion. Wagle has been seeking support from the UN's Loss and Damage Fund.

The Post, citing TIME, reported that on October 22 the UN gave provisional approval for $21.6 million from the fund for Nepal, described as the fund's first use.

## Who else is on the list

This is TIME's fourth annual climate list. Alongside Wagle in the "Leaders" category are UN Secretary-General António Guterres, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, Britain's Prince William and former Samoan Prime Minister Fiamē Naomi Mataʻafa. The governors of Delaware and Utah are also in this category.

TIME Editor-in-Chief Sam Jacobs said editors and reporters reviewed hundreds of names and chose people whose work over the past 12 months was measurable and lasting. The Kathmandu Post notes that TIME's profile page for Wagle labels the content as sponsored, produced in partnership with a company called Ally.

## Off to COP31

On the same day as the announcement, Wagle left for Türkiye to attend COP31, the UN climate conference, which opened Monday in Antalya. Nepal, together with Bhutan, has already called for warming in the Himalayan region to be treated as a separate agenda item. How far the compensation demand advances at the conference remains to be seen.
