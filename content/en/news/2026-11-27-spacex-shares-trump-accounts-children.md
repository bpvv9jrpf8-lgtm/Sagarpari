Nepali families in the U.S. with young children may want to pay attention: **SpaceX** says it will put one newly issued share of its stock into every "currently active" **Trump Account**, and Elon Musk says he will personally match it. That means children with an active account would get two shares in total.

## What was announced

In a statement on Wednesday, Nov. 25, SpaceX said it would contribute "one newly issued share of its Class A common stock to every currently active Trump Account." Musk wrote on X: "Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from me personally."

According to Reuters, Musk said the company shares would be transferred next month. His own share will follow when his "lockup" period ends in June next year. Based on Wednesday's closing price of $127.44 a share, Reuters put the total value of the gift at about $18 billion. SpaceX President Gwynne Shotwell has separately announced a personal gift of more than 2 million SpaceX shares.

## What is a Trump Account?

Trump Accounts are tax-advantaged investment accounts for children. According to Fox Business, they are for American children under 18 with a valid **Social Security number (SSN)**. Children born between Jan. 1, 2025, and Dec. 31, 2028, are also eligible for a one-time $1,000 contribution from the federal government.

The Treasury Department has said about 70 million accounts have been set up for eligible children. But a parent or guardian must claim an account before they can manage it or add money, and it is not clear how many have been activated.

## What parents can do

- If your child has an SSN, check through official government channels whether an account has been created in their name and whether it is active.
- SpaceX's statement does not define "active" or say whether there is a cutoff date for accounts to qualify. SagarPari will update this story when more details are released.
- Don't give your or your child's SSN or bank details to unknown callers, emails or links that mention this giveaway.
