Nepal's investigation into money collected illegally from workers bound for Malaysia has now reached a former chief secretary, the country's top civil servant. Police's **Central Investigation Bureau (CIB)** has obtained a court warrant to arrest former chief secretary **Ek Narayan Aryal**.

A CIB official told The Kathmandu Post that Aryal has not been arrested yet. The bureau says its investigation found he was allegedly involved in a decision to send money collected illegally from workers to Malaysia.

## Six arrested a day earlier

On Wednesday, police arrested six people in the same case, including two former ministers of labour, employment and social security, **Sher Bahadur Kunwar** and **Rameshwar Raya Yadav**. The others are former ministry secretary **Binod KC**, former Employment Management Division heads **Umesh Dhungana** and **Rajiv Pokharel**, and former legal officer **Nirmala Gurung**.

## The allegations

The suspects are being investigated on charges related to **fraud and organized crime**. They are accused of taking part in a decision to send money collected unlawfully from workers to Malaysia in breach of an understanding between the two governments, of helping move foreign currency while bypassing contractual and legal procedures, and of failing to give defrauded workers legal remedies.

According to a CIB statement, the money sent amounted to **$8,370,439** and **£3,717,980**, or about **Rs 1.69 billion**.

These are allegations only; no one has been convicted by a court.

## What workers should know

The case is linked to extra fees charged to Malaysia-bound workers in the name of medical screening. If relatives or friends are preparing to work abroad, remind them to get a receipt for every fee and to report any demand above the official rate to Nepal's Department of Foreign Employment or the police.
