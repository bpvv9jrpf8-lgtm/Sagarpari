Nepal's men's cricket team is heading back to Lord's, the "Home of Cricket." Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC), which owns the London ground, announced on Thursday, November 19, that Nepal will play **Bangladesh in a men's T20 International at Lord's on Thursday, June 3, 2027**.

The match is part of an MCC-hosted Nepal tour of England. MCC says all the matches will be ICC-recognized official internationals, sanctioned by the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB).

## Why it's historic

- It will be Nepal's **first match at Lord's against an ICC Full Member** nation.
- It will be Bangladesh men's **first T20I at Lord's**.
- A Middlesex T20 Blast match will follow at Lord's later the same day. MCC says it is the first men's international and domestic double-header at the ground.

Nepal last played a T20I at Lord's in 2018, facing the Netherlands in an MCC-hosted triple-header. Bangladesh, meanwhile, plays a Test against England at Lord's in late May 2027, its first Test at the ground in 17 years.

## Tour schedule

According to MCC:

- **Tue, June 1:** Nepal vs Scotland, Wormsley, 50 overs
- **Thu, June 3:** Bangladesh vs Nepal, Lord's, T20
- **Sun, June 6:** Ireland vs Nepal, Wormsley, 50 overs
- **Wed, June 9:** Ireland vs Nepal, Wormsley, T20
- **Fri, June 11:** Ireland vs Nepal, Beckenham, T20
- **Sat, June 12:** Ireland vs Nepal, Beckenham, T20

Start times and ticket details will be announced later, MCC said.

## What they said

Paras Khadka, secretary of the Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN), said returning to Lord's is always "a moment of immense pride and honour." CAN president Chatur Bahadur Chand noted it will be Nepal's first match at the ground against a Full Member.

MCC chief executive and secretary Robert Lawson welcomed the fact that the game follows soon after Bangladesh's first Lord's Test in 17 years. Bangladesh Cricket Board president Tamim Iqbal said it will be the first time his country's men's team plays a T20I at the venue.

For Nepali fans in the UK and Europe, and supporters traveling from the US, the tour offers a rare chance to watch the national team up close.
