Nepal's cricket team is heading back to Lord's, the London ground known as "the home of cricket." Nepal and Bangladesh will play a men's T20 International (T20I) there in June 2027.

The Marylebone Cricket Club (MCC), the Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN) and the Bangladesh Cricket Board (BCB) announced the match on Thursday at a joint press conference at Tribhuvan University International Cricket Stadium in Kirtipur. According to The Kathmandu Post, MCC chief executive and secretary Robert Lawson, CAN president Chatur Bahadur Chand, CAN secretary Paras Khadka and British Ambassador to Nepal Rob Fenn attended.

## Why it's historic

- It will be Bangladesh's first men's T20I at Lord's.
- It is also the first time Nepal will play an ICC Full Member at Lord's. Nepal previously played at the ground in 2018, in a tri-series organized by MCC.
- A Middlesex T20 Blast match will follow on the same day, described as the first men's international and domestic double-header at Lord's.

Bangladesh plays a Test against England at Lord's from May 28 to June 1, according to the ground's official fixture list — its first Test there in 17 years. The Nepal match comes shortly after. The exact date of the T20I was not given in the reports.

## Rest of the England tour

According to the Post, Nepal's tour of England, hosted by MCC, also includes:

- **June 1:** One-Day International vs Scotland, Wormsley
- **June 9:** 50-over match vs Ireland, Wormsley
- **June 11 and 12:** Two T20Is vs Ireland, Beckenham

The matches are ICC-recognized internationals sanctioned by the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB). MCC says the Lord's game will be shown live on TV in Nepal and Bangladesh.

## What they said

Lawson said MCC was thrilled the match would follow so soon after Bangladesh's first Lord's Test in 17 years, and thanked CAN, the BCB, Cricket Ireland, Cricket Scotland and the ECB. Khadka said returning to Lord's "is always a moment of immense pride and honour."

Before that, Nepal and Bangladesh meet in a three-match T20I series in Kathmandu in March 2027. According to the ICC, it is the first bilateral series between the two men's teams, starting March 26.

Ticket details for fans in the UK, Europe or traveling from the US have not been announced yet.
