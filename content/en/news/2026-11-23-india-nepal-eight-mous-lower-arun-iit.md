Nepal and India signed eight memorandums of understanding (MoUs) in New Delhi on Monday during Prime Minister Balendra Shah's visit to India. The two prime ministers virtually laid the foundation stone for the 669-megawatt Lower Arun Hydropower Project, and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced that India will set up an offshore campus of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) in Nepal.

India also announced a development cooperation package of 24,000 crore Nepali rupees (Rs 240 billion) for post-flood reconstruction and long-term development. The two countries also issued a joint vision document for a "Viksit Bharat and Samunnat Nepal."

## The agreements

According to Khabarhub, the MoUs cover:

- Academic and research cooperation between India's National Forensic Sciences University in Gujarat and the Nepal Police's Crime Investigation Department
- Police training cooperation between India's Rashtriya Raksha University and Nepal's National Police Academy
- Capacity building for Nepali civil servants between India's National Centre for Good Governance and Nepal Administrative Staff College
- Separate agreements on Ayurveda and traditional medicine, sustainable urban development, health and medicine, and livestock and fisheries development
- A satellite communication network using the South Asia Satellite, between Nepal's Ministry of Communication and Information Technology and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)

## Projects opened and launched

The two leaders jointly and virtually inaugurated a motorable bridge linking Darchula in Nepal with Dharchula in India, and the second phase of the Motihari–Amlekhgunj petroleum pipeline. Besides Lower Arun, they laid foundation stones for a new petroleum storage terminal in Jhapa and an integrated check post at Banbasa in India's Uttarakhand state.

They also virtually flagged off the Saryu–Kamala Express, a train linking Janakpurdham and Ayodhya. According to IANS, regular weekly service begins November 28.

## IIT campus and scholarships

In a joint press statement at Hyderabad House, Modi said India will establish a startup incubator, an innovation hub and an IIT offshore campus in Nepal, ANI reported. No location or timeline for the campus has been announced. Modi also said India will expand scholarships in India for Nepali students in law and technology, work with Nepal on an agro-industrial park, and step up cricket cooperation, including infrastructure and player training.

These sources do not report any decision on the additional air entry routes Nepal has sought through Indian airspace.

Shah arrived in Delhi on Sunday on his first bilateral foreign trip since taking office in March.
