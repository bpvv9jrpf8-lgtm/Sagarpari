Nepal's 10th National Games, hosted by Karnali Province, got underway on Tuesday with competition in five sports. Two national records fell on the first day of swimming in Nepalgunj, Banke — one of them set by 15-year-old Lily Bohra, who swims for the Non-Resident Nepali Association (NRNA) team.

According to The Kathmandu Post, this edition features 384 events across 32 sports. The formal opening ceremony is set for December 2 in Surkhet, and the closing ceremony is scheduled for December 13.

## Two records in the pool

In the women's 400m individual medley, Bohra touched in 5 minutes 17.01 seconds. The previous national record, 5:48.54, had stood since 2016, when it was set by Sofia Gardegaard Shah. Army's Duana Lama finished second and Police's Devanshi Joshi third.

In the men's 50m breaststroke, 22-year-old Bikash Kumal of Koshi Province lowered his own national record to 30.64 seconds, from 30.66 seconds set in June 2023, and took the gold medal.

Eleven men's and eleven women's swimming events were completed on Tuesday. Bagmati Province won the most golds, with NRNA swimmers also near the top of the medal table. The Post reported that hosts Karnali, along with Madhesh, Lumbini, Gandaki and the Armed Police Force (APF), did not win a swimming medal on day one.

## Other results

Swimming and tennis are being held outside Karnali; tennis is in Gulariya, Bardiya. Karnali hosted football, cricket and basketball on the opening day.

- **Men's football:** Nepal Police Club beat hosts Karnali 2-0, and Madhesh edged Sudurpashchim 1-0.
- **Women's cricket:** Army beat Koshi by six wickets, and APF beat Lumbini by 158 runs.
- **Women's basketball (5x5):** APF beat Police 52-19, and Sudurpashchim beat Bagmati 36-33.

On Wednesday, women's cricket features Bagmati vs. Sudurpashchim and Koshi vs. Karnali, while the men's football schedule has Army vs. Lumbini and Koshi vs. Bagmati.

## Why it matters to the diaspora

The NRNA has entered its own team at the National Games, and a diaspora athlete set a national record on the very first day. Of the 32 sports, 23 will be held in Surkhet, with the rest spread across other districts of Karnali and Lumbini.
