The **Saryu-Kamala Express**, the weekly train linking Janakpurdham in Nepal with Ayodhya in India, opens to regular passengers this **Saturday (Nov. 28)** — and fares have now been announced.

According to Ratopati and Nepal News, India's Ministry of Railways has set fares for four classes. Ticket sales at Janakpurdham Railway Station began Thursday afternoon, while Indian Railways had already opened online booking.

## Fares

Fares are in Indian rupees (INR):

- **Sleeper:** INR 365
- **AC 3-tier Economy:** INR 885
- **AC 3-tier:** INR 965
- **AC 2-tier:** INR 1,360

## Schedule and booking

The train runs once a week: it leaves **Janakpurdham every Saturday** and returns from **Ayodhya every Monday**. It has **17 coaches** — 7 sleeper, 3 AC 3-tier, 3 AC 3-tier Economy, 2 AC 2-tier and 2 luggage/guard (SLR) coaches.

Buyers in Nepal need to go to the Janakpur station in person. Ratopati reports that Nepali citizens can buy tickets and travel by showing one government-issued ID: a **citizenship certificate, passport or voter ID card**.

Exact departure and arrival times were not given in the reports. **Paribesh Parajuli**, managing director of Nepal Railway Company, said some technical work still remains.

## Background

Prime Minister **Balendra Shah** and the Indian prime minister jointly inaugurated the service during Shah's visit to India. When the route was first approved, fares and ticketing details had not yet been released. The line from Janakpurdham to Ayodhya Cantt covers about 604 km.

In Hindu tradition, Janakpur is the birthplace of Sita and Ayodhya the birthplace of Ram, and the service is expected to boost religious tourism. For Nepalis in the U.S. planning a pilgrimage during a trip home, it offers an inexpensive rail option. The sources do not say what rules apply to travelers holding foreign passports, so check with Nepal Railway Company or Indian Railways before you go.
