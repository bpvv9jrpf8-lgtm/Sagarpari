Chhath, the four-day festival dedicated to the sun god and Chhathi Maiya, began on Friday, Kartik 27 (November 13). Coming right after Deepawali, the festival has brought new energy to villages and towns across Nepal, especially in the Mithila region, Radio Nepal reported. Devotional songs to the sun god and Chhathi Maiya are playing in homes, and devotees are busy cleaning the banks of rivers, ponds and other water bodies to build and decorate Chhath ghats.

## The four days

According to Radio Nepal, the festival runs from Chaturthi to Saptami. The first day is Nahay-Khay: devotees bathe, clean their homes and begin the fast with a pure meal. The second day is Kharna. On the third and fourth days, offerings (arghya) are made to the setting and the rising sun.

NM Khabar reported that the main day, with the evening offering, is Sunday, Kartik 29 (November 15), and that it is a public holiday across Nepal. The festival ends on Monday, Kartik 30 (November 16), with the morning offering to the rising sun.

## Busy markets and ghats

Markets are crowded with people buying bananas, sugarcane, turmeric, ginger and the clay and bamboo items needed for worship, such as baskets and trays. Young people are busy setting up lights and decorations at the ghats.

In Janakpurdham, preparations have picked up because Prime Minister Balendra Shah is expected to observe Chhath there this year, NM Khabar reported. Ponds and ghats, including Gangasagar, are being cleaned and decorated. The timing and schedule of the prime minister's visit have not yet been made public.

Chhath places strong emphasis on purity, and it is widely seen as a festival that brings people together in shared faith and social harmony beyond caste and community lines. Madhesi and Terai-origin Nepali families in the United States are also preparing to mark Chhath at home and by local lakes and rivers.
