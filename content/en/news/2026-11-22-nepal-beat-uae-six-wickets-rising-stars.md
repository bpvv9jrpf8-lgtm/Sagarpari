Two days after being bowled out for 26 in a 306-run loss to India A, Nepal bounced back at the Asia Cup Rising Stars (50 overs). On Sunday, November 22, Nepal beat the United Arab Emirates by six wickets in a Group B match in Doha, Qatar.

According to The Kathmandu Post, the game was played at the University of Doha for Science and Technology ground.

## Lamichhane and Bhurtel strangle UAE

Nepal won the toss and chose to field. The bowlers never let the UAE settle, and their innings ended at 121 in 39.5 overs.

Captain Sandeep Lamichhane took 3 wickets for just 11 runs in his 10 overs, including three maidens. Kushal Bhurtel, better known for his batting, also took 3 wickets for 15 runs in 5 overs, with two maidens. Rashid Khan, Lalit Rajbanshi and Nandan Yadav took one wicket each.

Muhammad Shahdad top-scored for the UAE with 40 off 70 balls, and Harpreet Singh Bhatia added 32 off 48.

## Target reached in 22 overs

Chasing 122, Nepal reached 123 for 4 in just 22 overs. Bhurtel led the way with the bat too, scoring 57 off 56 balls with six fours and a six.

Basir Ahmad (18 not out off 31) and Pawan Sarraf (9 not out off 8) finished the job.

## After a record low

In their opening game on Friday, Nepal were bowled out for 26 in 11.3 overs against India A, described as the fifth-lowest total in List A cricket. Many of Nepal's regular players are missing the tournament because the Nepal Premier League (NPL) was running at the same time.

The win over the UAE is Nepal's first of the group stage. Group B includes India A, Pakistan A, Nepal and the UAE.

## Next up: Pakistan A

Nepal play their final group match against Pakistan A on Tuesday. For Nepali cricket fans in the US, that result will help decide where Nepal finish in the group.
