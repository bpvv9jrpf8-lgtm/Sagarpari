Traffic on the Prithvi Highway — the main road linking Kathmandu with Pokhara and the Terai — will be stopped for five hours every day at Ghoptebhir in Chitwan, on the Nagdhunga–Muglin section. Starting Saturday, Nov. 21, all vehicles will be held from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. daily until further notice.

## Why the closure

The Chitwan District Administration Office issued the notice on Friday. According to The Annapurna Express, it was issued by Assistant Chief District Officer Ishwari Prasad Sharma.

Repeated dry landslides at Ghoptebhir have blocked the road again and again. Officials say rocks and soil keep falling from the slope above the road, so the closure window is needed to cut back the risky section and clear large rocks and debris. Earlier this month, just before Tihar, two landslides hit the same spot within 24 hours and left travelers stuck for hours.

## Where vehicles will be stopped

According to the District Traffic Police Office in Chitwan, vehicles will be held before the closure begins at these points:

- Coming from Pokhara: Aabukhaireni
- Coming from Narayangadh: Jugedi and Aaptari
- Coming from the Kathmandu side: Ramailo Danda

Anil Thapa, chief of the Chitwan District Traffic Police Office, told Onlinekhabar that vehicles will not be stopped in Muglin Bazaar. He said it is not yet clear how many days the daily closures will continue. The administration has asked travelers to plan alternative arrangements.

## What it means for Nepalis in the US

Many Nepali Americans travel home during the winter wedding and holiday season, and family members in Nepal often travel between Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan. If you or your family will use this road, plan to pass Ghoptebhir before 11 a.m. or after 4 p.m. If you need to catch a flight from Kathmandu's Tribhuvan International Airport, leave extra time around the closure window. Check the latest traffic police updates before setting out, since landslides can change conditions quickly.
