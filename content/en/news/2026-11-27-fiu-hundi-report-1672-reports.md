Hundi, the informal system for sending money home from abroad, has grown into an organized network that runs through bank accounts, digital wallets, remittance sub-agents and businesses, according to Nepal Rastra Bank's **Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-Nepal)**. The findings are in its "Strategic Analysis Report on Hundi, 2026," published in November.

## 1,672 reports in 18 months

Hundi became a predicate offence under Nepal's anti-money-laundering law on Jan. 2, 2025. Between that date and July 16, 2026, banks and other reporting institutions filed **15,815** suspicious transaction and activity reports with the FIU. Of those, **1,672** (about 10.6%) were linked to hundi.

Commercial banks filed 1,503 of the hundi reports, or about 90%. The report notes this reflects who filed the reports, not who carried out the transactions. A suspicious transaction report is also not proof of a crime.

## Madhesh Province leads

The FIU studied a random sample of 200 of the 1,672 reports. About **53% of the people in the sample were from Madhesh Province**, followed by Koshi (16%) and Lumbini (12.5%). Siraha (10.5%) and Saptari (10%) topped the districts. The report says most are border districts that send large numbers of migrant workers abroad.

In the sample, 84% were men and 65.5% were aged 25 to 44. About 40% of the reports came within a year of the account being opened.

## Where the money comes from, and how

The report says hundi money comes from Gulf countries, Malaysia, South Korea, Japan and various European countries. Funds collected abroad reach families in Nepal through local bank accounts and wallets.

The FIU identified eight main methods:

- "Mule" accounts opened in other people's names
- Splitting cash into small deposits across several accounts
- Front and shell companies
- Cryptocurrency
- Carrying cash physically
- Coordinating through digital apps while settling the traditional way
- Remittance sub-agents
- Links to gold smuggling

## Enforcement rising

Over the past five fiscal years, the report counts 197 hundi investigations, 190 cases filed and 103 final verdicts. Police investigations rose from 18 in fiscal year 2078/79 to 60 in 2081/82.

## What it means for readers in the U.S.

Hundi is illegal in Nepal. The report recommends reducing the incentives to use informal channels, tighter oversight of digital wallets and remittance agents, and stronger cash controls at the border. It also shows that letting someone else use your account can put it under investigation as a "mule" account. Licensed banks and remittance companies are the safe way to send money home from the U.S.
