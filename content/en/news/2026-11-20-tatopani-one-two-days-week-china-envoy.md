The Tatopani border crossing between Nepal and China is set to reopen on a limited basis next week. Chinese Ambassador to Nepal Zhang Maoming said preparations are underway to operate the checkpoint one or two days a week, but that running it regularly is not possible for now.

Zhang made the remarks Thursday at an interaction with newspaper and media editors in Kathmandu. He said the one-or-two-day schedule would continue until an alternative arrangement is in place.

## Why not a full reopening?

Zhang called Tatopani, on the Araniko Highway, a symbol of Nepal–China friendship, but said the terrain around the checkpoint is risky, which makes regular operation difficult. The specific days it will open have not yet been announced.

Just two days earlier, Foreign Minister Shishir Khanal had asked the Chinese side to reopen Tatopani immediately. It is the closest Chinese border point to Kathmandu. The crossing has repeatedly closed and reopened, and traders say that has made goods from China slower and more expensive to bring in.

## Rasuwagadhi and Korala

On Rasuwagadhi, another crossing close to Kathmandu that is currently not operating, Zhang said China will move ahead with the process once it receives a formal proposal from the Government of Nepal.

He also said the road linking the Korala border point in Mustang to Jomsom is being upgraded and blacktopped.

## China's aid priorities

Zhang said China will keep supporting Nepal, with priority on health, education, road networks, industry, trade and energy. He said Nepal has always been a priority for China and that Beijing is ready to support Nepal's economic development and prosperity.

He also praised Nepal's political stability and anti-corruption efforts, and briefed editors on the Chinese Communist Party Central Committee meeting in Beijing and the APEC meeting that concluded in Shenzhen on Thursday.

## What it means

A partial reopening should ease some of the backlog of goods waiting on the Chinese side, but one or two days a week falls well short of full capacity. With other high-altitude crossings unable to run fully in winter, traders are watching closely how Tatopani operates. SagarPari will update once the schedule is announced.
