The long-planned train linking **Janakpur Dham** in Nepal, revered as the birthplace of Sita, with **Ayodhya** in India has received final approval. Indian Railways has approved a new **weekly train service** between the two pilgrimage cities, according to an announcement made public on Saturday, November 14.

Indian news agency IANS reported that the announcement came through India's Press Information Bureau (PIB). Earlier reports had said preparations were in their final stage and a trial run had taken place, but no schedule or launch date had been set.

## When and where it runs

According to IANS:

- The train will leave Janakpur Dham for Ayodhya **every Saturday**.
- The return trip will leave Ayodhya Cantt. **every Monday**.
- The route between Janakpur Dham and Ayodhya Cantt. is about **604 kilometers**.
- Commercial stops along the way: Jaynagar, Madhubani, Shisho, Sitamarhi, Raxaul, Narkatiaganj, Gorakhpur, Basti, Mankapur and Ayodhya Dham.

The train passes through towns in the Indian states of Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. Departure and arrival times, fares and booking details have not been announced yet.

## Launch on November 23

According to Nepal News, the service is scheduled to be formally inaugurated on **November 23**. Prime Minister **Balendra Shah** and Indian Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** are expected to launch it jointly by video conference. The event is being planned as Shah prepares for a visit to India; neither government has formally released the full itinerary of that visit.

## Why it matters

Janakpur and Ayodhya share deep religious and cultural ties. In Hindu tradition, Janakpur is the birthplace of Sita and Ayodhya the birthplace of Ram. IANS reported that the service is expected to help religious tourism, students, business travelers and families, and to benefit hotels, transport operators and small traders along the route.
