Nepali runner **Santoshi Shrestha** won two gold medals at the **Dhiraagu Road Race 2026** in the Maldives, taking both the women's **half marathon** and the women's **5km** — and setting a new course record in each.

According to The Kathmandu Post, Shrestha finished the half marathon in **1 hour, 26 minutes and 39 seconds**. Her 5km time was not reported. She also won both races at the same event two years ago.

## About the race

The 17th edition of the race was held at Central Park in Hulhumalé, Maldives, with about 5,500 runners from several countries. The event is recognized by AIMS (the Association of International Marathons and Distance Races) and World Athletics. Four new categories were added this year.

In the women's half marathon, Aishath Jinanee Ibrahim of the Maldives finished second and Fathimath Siza, also of the Maldives, was third.

## Two silvers for Nepal in the men's races

In the men's half marathon, defending champion **Santosh Bikram Bista** of Nepal finished second, just one second behind Kenya's Tairu Chege, who won in 1:18:16. Shifaz Mohamed of the Maldives was third. Chege also won the men's 5km, with Bista again second.

In the open 10km, Hussain Fazil Haroon of the Maldives won the men's race and Aishath Rizmee won the women's. Masters titles went to Karim Makok and Zara Riyaz (10km) and to Aswan Ali and Husna Mohamed (half marathon).

## Prize money

The total prize purse was 296,000 Maldivian rufiyaa. Half-marathon winners received 25,000 rufiyaa, with 15,000 for second and 10,000 for third. In the 5km, gold, silver and bronze paid 10,000, 8,000 and 6,000 rufiyaa. The Kathmandu Post put Shrestha's total winnings from her two races at about $2,300.
