Families who lost their homes in the August 26 Bhotekoshi flood could receive up to **Rs 800,000** each to build new ones. Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority decided at a meeting on Thursday, November 26, to recommend one-time rebuilding grants, The Kathmandu Post reported.

## How much, and where

Under the recommendation, the grant depends on where a family rebuilds:

- **Mountain areas:** Rs 800,000
- **Hill areas:** Rs 700,000
- **Tarai (plains):** Rs 600,000

The assistance is meant to cover Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading and other districts hit by the flood.

## What about families who lost their land?

Families who still own land in their district would get the grant to rebuild on their existing plots. Families whose land was washed away or can no longer be used would be housed under an **integrated housing programme**, according to a ministry source quoted by the Post.

## What happens next

The Ministry of Infrastructure Development has already drafted a proposal based on the authority's decision. A ministry source told the Post the proposal is ready to go to the Cabinet, which is expected to decide on it at its next meeting. Until the Cabinet approves it, the amounts are not final.

Thursday's meeting was attended by Infrastructure Development Minister Sunil Lamsal, Home Minister Sudhan Gurung, Minister for Land Management, Cooperatives, Federal Affairs and General Administration Pratibha Rawal, and Health and Food Hygiene Minister Nisha Mehta.

## Why it matters

Three months after the flood, many families in Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading are still in temporary shelters. Setting the rebuilding amount is a key step toward permanent housing. On the same day, the government's disaster executive committee also recommended declaring 3,915 people missing in the flood dead.

Many Nepalis in the US have family in the affected districts. Details on how to apply, what documents are needed and the timeline are expected after the Cabinet decides; relatives should watch for notices from the local municipality and the District Administration Office.
