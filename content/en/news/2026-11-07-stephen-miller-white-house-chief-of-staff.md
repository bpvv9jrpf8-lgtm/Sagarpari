President Donald Trump has named Stephen Miller, widely seen as the chief architect of his administration's hardline immigration and deportation policies, as White House chief of staff. Miller replaces Susie Wiles. It is the biggest shake-up of Trump's team since the midterm elections.

## What happened

Trump made the announcement on Friday, November 6, on his social media platform Truth Social. According to CBS News, Trump wrote that Miller, currently deputy chief of staff, will become chief of staff starting Monday, November 9. Trump called Miller one of his "most trusted advisers."

Trump said Wiles will move to the Trump Political Operation. Citing a U.S. official, CBS reported that Wiles submitted her resignation to the president on Thursday night. On X, Wiles wrote that while her role is changing, her commitment to the president and the movement will never change. Wiles ran Trump's 2024 campaign and had served as chief of staff since the first day of his second term.

## Why it matters for immigrants

According to CBS and Al Jazeera, Miller has been one of Trump's closest advisers across both terms. He is known as the main planner behind the administration's strict border controls, immigration enforcement and deportation program. Al Jazeera noted that immigration was also a major issue in the midterm elections.

The chief of staff runs the White House's day-to-day operations, staff and policy priorities. CBS wrote that the appointment is being read as a sign that Trump is doubling down on his current approach to governing. However, neither report mentions any specific new policy on H-1B visas, green cards or anything else.

According to Al Jazeera, Democrats are widely expected to open investigations into Miller after they take control of the House and Senate in January.

## Open to immigration talks with Democrats

Meanwhile, speaking to reporters in Florida, Trump signaled he could work with Democrats on immigration and taxes. According to the Indian news agency ANI, he said, as he had before, that immigration and tax cuts were among the areas where they could work together. He did not give details of any specific bill or proposal.
