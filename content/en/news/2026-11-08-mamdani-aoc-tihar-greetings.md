As Nepali communities across the United States celebrated Kukur Tihar and Laxmi Puja, New York City Mayor **Zohran Mamdani** and U.S. Rep. **Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez** greeted the community in Nepali. Both posted separate messages on social media wishing everyone "Tihar ko hardik shubhakamana" — warm Tihar greetings.

## Mamdani: "Tihar ko hardik shuvakamana!"

Mayor Mamdani opened his message on the mayor's official X account in Nepali — "Tihar ko hardik shuvakamana!" He then wrote in English that as thousands of Nepali New Yorkers light diyos for Laxmi Puja and celebrate the bond of Bhai Tika, he hoped this Tihar would bring everyone prosperity, peace and a sense of belonging.

Community members said that naming Laxmi Puja and Bhai Tika showed his familiarity with what the festival means. His message was viewed and shared thousands of times within a short period.

## Ocasio-Cortez: "To our Nepali neighbors"

Rep. Ocasio-Cortez wrote on her official Facebook page: "Tihar ko hardik shubhakamana to our Nepali neighbors!" She said Tihar reminds us to honor every bond that sustains us — from the animals who share our lives to the siblings who protect us — and wished everyone happiness, prosperity and peace.

Her mention of animals and siblings pointed to Kukur Tihar and Bhai Tika. The Jackson Heights–Elmhurst area of Queens, in her district, is home to one of the largest Nepali communities in the United States, so her greeting reached her own constituents directly.

## Growing recognition for the diaspora

Just a day earlier, [Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese wore a Dhaka topi and celebrated Tihar with the Nepali community at the Nepal Festival in Sydney](https://sagarpari.com/news/albanese-dhaka-topi-sydney-nepal-festival/) (story in Nepali). Leaders of major cities around the world openly recognizing a Nepali festival within a single week shows the growing presence and visibility of Nepali communities abroad.

Tihar, the biggest festival after Dashain, is being celebrated by Nepalis in cities across America with diyos at home, deusi-bhailo and cultural programs, and the community is now getting ready for Bhai Tika.
