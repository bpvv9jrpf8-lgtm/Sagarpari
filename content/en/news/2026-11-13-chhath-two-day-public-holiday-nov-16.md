Nepal will have **two public holidays** for the Chhath festival this year. In addition to the holiday already announced for **Sunday, November 15**, the government has declared **Monday, November 16**, the day after Chhath, a public holiday as well.

The Ministry of Home Affairs announced the extra day in a notice on Friday, November 13, according to Nepal News and The Kathmandu Post.

## Why the extra day?

Home Ministry spokesperson and joint secretary **Ananda Kafle** told Nepal News the additional holiday is meant to accommodate the festival's rituals. On the first evening, devotees offer prayers (argha) to the setting sun. The next morning they pray to the rising sun and break their fast.

According to a Kathmandu Post editorial, the federal holiday calendar has until now covered only the evening offering, not the morning one.

## A growing festival

Chhath is a four-day observance that includes a roughly 36-hour fast without water. In an editorial published Thursday, The Kathmandu Post argued that Chhath has spread well beyond the Madhesh region, but state support has not kept pace. The editorial noted that last year the federal government gave a single holiday, while Madhesh Province added a second day.

This year, the federal government itself is covering both the evening and morning prayers.

## What it means

Government offices in Nepal generally close on public holidays. If you need any government work done in Nepal, plan around November 15 and 16. Banks, schools and private businesses will announce their own schedules.

## For Nepalis in the US

Many Nepalis in the US rely on family members back home to handle land office, ward office or other paperwork. Because Nepal is many hours ahead of the US, the holidays there begin during Saturday night and Sunday US time. If you are sending documents or waiting on a government office in Nepal, expect those two days to be closed.
