**SagarPari** (सागरपारि, "across the ocean") is an online news site for Nepalis living in the United States. Our main edition is in Nepali; this English edition carries our most useful stories in English for readers who prefer it, for the second generation, and for anyone following the Nepali community in America.

Our goal is simple: deliver the information you need while living in the US (visas, work, taxes, community) and the main news from Nepal, in plain language and with reliable sources.

## Our sections

- [Immigration](/en/category/immigration/) — visas, green cards, TPS, citizenship and USCIS updates.
- [Jobs](/en/category/jobs/) — work, wage rules, workers' rights and business.
- [Money & Taxes](/en/category/money/) — taxes, remittances, banking, insurance and household costs.
- [Community](/en/category/community/) — events, achievements and news from Nepali communities across the US.
- [Nepal](/en/category/nepal/) — the main news from Nepal, short and clear.
- [Trending](/en/category/trending/) — the US and world stories people are talking about.

## How we work

- Every story names its sources.
- Immigration and legal stories are written from official sources only.
- We do not give legal advice. For your own case, consult an accredited immigration attorney.
- Mistakes are fixed under our [corrections policy](/en/corrections/).

To share news, tips or advertising inquiries, see our [contact page](/en/contact/).
