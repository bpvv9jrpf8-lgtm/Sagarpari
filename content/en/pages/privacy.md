Last updated: November 12, 2026

You do not need an account or login to read SagarPari (sagarpari.com). We do not sell your personal information, and because there are no accounts, we do not collect readers' names, addresses or phone numbers. This policy explains what information may be used to run the site and how.

## What we may see

- Our hosting and security provider (such as Cloudflare) may keep basic technical information in server logs — IP address, browser type, pages viewed and time. This is used to keep the site secure and running.
- If you contact us by email, we keep your email address and message only in order to reply.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files that a site or connected services store in your browser. Cookies may be used to understand how the site is used and to show ads.

You can block or delete cookies, or have your browser warn you before they are stored, in your browser settings. Turning cookies off does not affect your ability to read our news.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how many people visit and which stories are read most. This data is viewed only in aggregate; individual readers are not identified.

## Advertising — Google AdSense and third parties

This site uses, or may use, advertising services including Google AdSense. In that case:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies (including DoubleClick) enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You can read more about how Google uses cookies for advertising in [Google's advertising policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## How to opt out of personalized ads

If you do not want interest-based (personalized) advertising:

- Visit [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and turn off ad personalization.
- Use the [aboutads.info choices page](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) to opt out of personalized ads from other participating companies.

These choices do not remove ads; you will still see ads, but they will not be chosen based on your interests.

## Links to other sites

Our stories may link to government or other external sites. SagarPari is not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please read their own policies.

## Children's privacy

This site is for a general audience and is not directed at collecting information from children under 13. If a parent learns that their child has sent us personal information, please email us below and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, write to **info@sagarpari.com**.
